I've always been a window seat person.

<p class="indented-stanza">
40,000 feet above the surface of Earth,
<br/>
85% the speed of sound,
<br/>
A journey of over 8000 miles, complete in hours,
<br/>
The same journey that sailors trudged through for months.
<br/>
How small our world has become!
</p>

One side of my neck starts to hurt as I gaze outside.

<p class="indented-stanza">
The ground grows smaller,
<br/>
Stratus clouds resemble a mystical Antarctic landscape,
<br/>
Cumulonimbus clouds birth lightning,
<br/>
Millions of stars shine in the middle of the ocean.
<br/>
How vast our universe really is!
</p>

But lately, I've been afraid of becoming an aisle seat passenger.

<p class="indented-stanza">
Neck free of stiffness,
<br/>
Eyemask on during taxi, takeoff, and landing,
<br/>
Headphones in as the pilot flying hits TO/GA,
<br/>
Blissfully unaware of the view.
</p>
